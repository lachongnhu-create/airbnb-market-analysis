# Airbnb Market Analysis

## Project Overview

Analysed over 250,000 Airbnb listings and 5 million reviews to identify the global airbnb market, pricing drivers, traveller demand, and investment opportunities.

## Business Questions

- Which Airbnb markets are expensive, affordable, or highly competitive?
- Which cities are most profitable?
- What factors influence Airbnb prices?
- How does demand change throughout the year?
- Which neighbourhoods are best for investment?

## Dataset

- 250K+ listings 
- 5M+ reviews
- Multiple international cities

## Key Findings

- Property size and amenities significantly influence pricing.
- Demand declined during COVID-19 and recovered afterward.
- Several neighbourhoods showed high demand despite relatively low supply.
